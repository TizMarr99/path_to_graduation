### Prompt finale

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: collectible magical artifact inventory icon for narrative game, single square PNG with genuinely transparent background. Reference 1 (guitar artifact) establishes the ornate gold collectible presentation and purple/teal colors; reference 2 (film reel) establishes the rich aged metal texture, precious cinematic object and warm rim lighting. Create a NEW distinct artifact: SPECCHIO PRISMATICO, an antique handheld oval mirror, complete carved handle, elegant baroque champagne-gold brass frame with restrained leaf ornament, a small dark plum jewel at the joint. Upright, slightly angled in three-quarter view, centered, occupying most of the canvas with safe margins and nothing cropped. The mirror surface is intact faceted smoked silver crystal, reflecting abstract petrol teal and plum shadows with subtle spectral prismatic glints and a warm ivory flash: elegant, mysterious, quietly uncanny. Make the silhouette and main shapes legible at 78x78 pixels; refined detail but not a cluttered scene. Decadent couture atelier heirloom, painterly semi-realistic premium game prop, consistent with both supplied artifact references. No room, scenery, floor, pedestal, hands, faces, people or additional objects. No surrounding medal or plaque. No lettering, labels, logos, watermark, letters or words; its name is rendered separately by the interface. Gold bevel highlights and restrained violet reflections; no huge outer glow. Everything outside the solid mirror and frame must be transparent, no white/black backdrop and no checkerboard drawn into image.

Immediate onset, total duration 3 seconds, natural decay. No music, voices, screams, repeated clicks or long intro.

### Musica ambientale — Suno / Instrumental

Instrumental ambient soundtrack for a mysterious abandoned couture atelier. Decadent elegance, subtle unease. Slow sparse waltz in 3/4, 60 BPM, felt piano, delicate music-box celesta, soft low strings and faint glass harmonics. Intimate, restrained, slightly melancholic, spacious and quietly uncanny.
